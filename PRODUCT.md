# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Athletes following personal and coach-assigned training on phones, tablets, and desktop computers. Coaches manage reusable workouts and team assignments.

## Product Purpose

MENTA connects an athlete's development plan, training, performance, and team context. The training flow should make the next action clear and preserve work through interruptions.

## Brand Personality

Simple, clean, purposeful. Preserve the existing white surfaces, system typography, and restrained monochrome actions. The requested rounded workout sheet belongs to MENTA and must use clear workout language.

## Anti-references

Avoid imitating system purchases, authentication panels, or payment confirmation. Avoid decorative dashboard complexity and invented coaching recommendations.

## Design Principles

- Review the actual workout and instructions before starting.
- Starting, resuming, and saving are separate, explicit actions.
- Show storage and network failures with a useful recovery action.
- Preserve existing onboarding and Today-screen ownership for separate work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide a button alternative to swiping, keyboard focus management, readable contrast, reduced motion, safe-area spacing, and layouts suitable for touch and zoom.
