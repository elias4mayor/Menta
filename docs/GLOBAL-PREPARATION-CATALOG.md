# MENTA global preparation and onboarding catalog

Version 1 · 2026-10-16 · 63 registered sports including Other/custom · 21 onboarding personas.

Planning guidance, not assigned tasks, official rules, eligibility approval or medical clearance. Local organizers and authorized staff approve event requirements. Choosing a guide never changes your account role or permissions.

## Ownership and current implementation

Codex owns native iOS. Claude owns website/shared backend and onboarding APIs. This catalog is the shared content contract, not a new permission system. The native guide is reference content: it does not complete onboarding, assign tasks, approve eligibility, provide clinical decisions or mark an event safe. The current game-day API models two-team scores; it does not yet implement all formats listed below. Website source changes in this phase are limited to documentation/data so concurrent Claude implementation is preserved.

## Global setup

### Location, language and time

- Confirm your country/region, preferred language and the venue time zone.
- Check the venue’s local time and any time difference when traveling.
- Confirm your preferred language, date/time display and any communication-access needs.

### Sport and competition identity

- Choose sport, discipline, format, team/individual entry and current season.
- Allow multiple sports/teams and a custom sport; distinguish soccer from American football and hockey disciplines.
- Attach the actual local federation/league, rule edition and authorized organizer rather than a universal rule assumption.

### Units and categories

- Confirm the units used in your event’s results and your preferred measurement display.
- Confirm your competition category through the actual organizer where applicable; a profile is not an eligibility or classification decision.
- Confirm how the event is scored: times, distances, sets, judged routines, attempts, brackets or rankings.

### People and authority

- Confirm your account, team membership and approved staff/guardian relationships.
- Confirm your approved responsibilities for each team and who covers you if needed.
- Share information only with its intended audience; private roster, academic and care details do not belong on public event pages.

### Access and safeguarding

- Offer accessible transport, venue, communication and equipment-support requests through private channels.
- Use age-appropriate guardian and supervision workflows under local organizational policy.
- Configure local emergency contacts and qualified venue-specific response plans; do not hardcode one worldwide emergency number.

### Travel and connectivity

- Confirm domestic/international travel documents and consent through authorized organizers when applicable.
- Bring the latest organizer-approved itinerary and note when it was last updated.
- Confirm schedule changes, daylight-saving differences and overlapping events with affected people.

## Universal athlete preparation

### Before the event

- Confirm the correct event, entry, location, reporting time and organizer instructions.
- Review your assigned role, coach-approved preparation and any required authorized reviews.
- Confirm travel, school/work arrangements and who to contact if plans change.

### Pack and travel

- Check your sport-specific approved kit, spare items and labeled personal belongings.
- Confirm meals/hydration arrangements and any individual plan with the appropriate staff.
- Confirm meeting point, transport, arrival and the return/release arrangement.

### At the venue

- Check in through the organizer process and confirm schedule changes.
- Attend assigned equipment/credential checks and supervised warm-up.
- Know your coach/support contacts and the venue briefing; report concerns to appropriate staff.

### After the event

- Follow staff-directed check-out, return travel and guardian release where applicable.
- Return issued equipment and report missing or damaged items.
- Review confirmed results and coach feedback; keep private support information out of public posts.

## Every onboarding persona

Keep the eight existing public account identities. The additional personas are approved duties/specializations; leadership remains invitation-only and fans can browse public pages without an account. One person can hold multiple approved team duties without creating another identity or receiving blanket access.

### Athlete / competitor

Account identity: ATHLETE. Access: Self-service identity; membership and participation reviewed separately.

Setup:

- Verify account and age-appropriate consent.
- Choose sport(s), discipline, role and optional measurements.
- Join the correct team/event and review sharing choices.

Preparation:

- Confirm your reporting, kit and coach-approved preparation.
- Review event updates and assigned tasks.
- Use the return/release process after the event.

First useful action: Open your next event and personal preparation guide.

## Competition-level overlays

Adaptive participation is supported across levels; it is not treated as a separate competition level. Apply the relevant accessibility and authorized classification process only where applicable.

### Youth / junior club

- Use age-appropriate supervision and local guardian-consent procedures.
- Confirm approved pickup/release and travel contacts.
- Use the actual junior competition format and participation rules.

### Middle school

- Prioritize adult supervision, school transport and approved guardian communication.
- Confirm locally required participation reviews through authorized staff.
- Keep student tasks simple and supervised; do not grant adult staff privileges.

### High / secondary school

- Coordinate classes, authorized eligibility reviews and event staffing.
- Name backups where one staff member holds multiple duties.
- Confirm school-specific travel, supervision and release procedures.

### College / university

- Coordinate departmental responsibilities, academic travel and credentials.
- Use the applicable association/federation and institutional review processes.
- Keep clinical, academic and public operations separate.

### Club / recreational

- Confirm organizer, age group, competition format and participant contacts.
- Assign essential operations even when staff are volunteers.
- Use event-specific arrangements rather than assuming school procedures apply.

### Professional / elite

- Coordinate department-specific run sheets, credentials and travel parties.
- Confirm applicable league/federation and contractual operating requirements.
- Use verified handovers and backups without exposing private athlete records.

### Masters / adult age-group

- Confirm event-authorized age group and entry process.
- Coordinate travel, schedule and individual support arrangements.
- Use the event’s actual rules and qualified review process.

## Formats and international expansion

Do not silently translate race times, judged scores, attempts, sets, stroke totals or multi-stage results into the current two-score API. Add versioned format-specific contracts and retain compatibility with the existing iOS game-day contract. ## Run-sheet contract for Claude

Use separate authenticated task endpoints from the public score endpoint. Each event task needs ID, event ID, department, title/instructions, owner, backup, due time, priority, status, version, actor/time history and an explicit local-review requirement where applicable. Support TODO, IN_PROGRESS, BLOCKED and COMPLETE without equating completion to eligibility or medical clearance. Team leadership sees authorized operations; assignees/backups see only permitted tasks; fans never receive private tasks. Current team membership and duty grants must be rechecked on every read/write/export. Keep clinical records and academic details in their existing restricted workflows. Publish an API contract before integrating native live task writes.
